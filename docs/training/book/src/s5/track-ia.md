# Track IA — Investment Advisory

> **Mirrors PGP T4(IA)-401/402/403 · Capstone: client financial plan (case-based)**

## Modules

1. **Overview of financial planning & wealth management** — goals, risk
   profiling, the plan document.
2. **Building blocks of FP/WM** — emergency fund, insurance, debt, investing,
   retirement, estate; product suitability.
3. **Risk management, practice & regulation** — SEBI IA regulations, suitability,
   fee disclosure, KYC/AML, records.

## Capstone

Write a **client financial plan** for a case-study family:

- Client risk profile → asset allocation recommendation.
- Product mapping (equity/debt/MF/insurance) with rationale.
- Suitability and compliance annex (why each product fits the client).
- A 10-year projection using SIP/return math.

## Lab surface

- `mutual_funds/02_sip_backtest.py` — SIP projections.
- `screening/*` and `mutual_funds/03_category_analysis.py` — product selection.
- `tantra.quant` — return and risk math for the plan.

## Out the other side

- IA exam-ready → degree requirement → IA registration path.
- Immediate careers: wealth associate, relationship manager, mutual fund
  distributor (NISM V-A / X-A pathways).
