# Career Pathways

## The three exits

```
Class 12 passout
   │
   ├── Technical / Trading ops (immediate)
   │      Terminal operator, trade support, technical analyst trainee
   │      Credentials: CFTe I, NISM VIII   (open to 12th pass)
   │
   ├── Advisory / Sales (immediate–1 yr)
   │      Wealth associate, relationship manager, MF distributor
   │      Credentials: NISM V-A, X-A, XV exam-ready
   │
   └── Research / Portfolio (exam-ready now, registered after degree)
          Equity research associate, investment advisor, portfolio analyst
          Credentials: NISM XV passed; graduate degree → SEBI RA/IA/PM registration
          Optional: CFTe II / CMT → international technical analyst credential
```

### The three exits, in detail

The salary bands below are **rough planning estimates** for Indian entry-level
roles in 2025–26, in rupees per year. Treat them as planning numbers, not
promises — actual offers vary with city, firm, and the skills you can
demonstrate in the interview.

| Exit | Entry roles | First-year pay (rough estimate) | Credentials that unlock it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical / Trading ops** | Terminal operator, trade support, technical analyst trainee, chartist | ₹2,40,000 – ₹3,60,000 per year | CFTe I + NISM VIII (both from end of S2) |
| **Advisory / Sales** | Wealth associate, relationship manager, MF distributor, client service | ₹2,40,000 – ₹3,60,000 per year plus incentives | NISM V-A / X-A; NISM XV exam-ready |
| **Research / Portfolio** | Equity research associate, investment advisor (post-degree), portfolio analyst | ₹3,00,000 – ₹4,80,000 per year (research associate) | NISM XV passed; graduate degree → SEBI RA/IA/PM registration |

These three exits are not a hierarchy you climb *instead of* the others — they
are the three doors open to a 12th-pass student. Many learners start in the top
row (technical/ops) while they finish the degree that unlocks the bottom row.

### What employers look for in each exit

- **Technical / Trading ops:** a CFTe I / NISM VIII scorecard, a chart portfolio
  with annotations you can explain aloud, and terminal familiarity from the
  S2-M8 labs.
- **Advisory / Sales:** the NISM V-A / X-A passes, a communication-lab record,
  and a paper-trading journal that proves you can explain a product's behaviour
  in plain words.
- **Research / Portfolio:** the S3 modeled-company workbook and the S4 research
  report, the NISM XV scorecard, and the ability to defend every number in your
  model line by line.

## Registration honesty (repeat of the front matter)

NISM examinations are open to Class 12 passouts. SEBI registration as
**RA / IA / PM** requires a graduate degree. This course makes you exam-ready
now and leaves the registration upgrade open; technical/trading/operations roles
are available immediately.

## Portfolio deliverables at exit (interview-ready)

1. S3 modeled-company Excel workbook (3-statement model + valuation)
2. S4 full research report on a live listed company
3. S5 track capstone (report / chart package / model portfolio / client plan)
4. Paper-trading journal + chart-pattern portfolio (S2)
5. Certification scorecards (CFTe I, NISM VIII, NISM XV)

## Timeline to employment

| Milestone | Time |
|---|---|
| CFTe I + NISM VIII passed | ~8 months (end of S2) |
| Paper-trading journal + terminal certification | ~8 months |
| NISM XV passed | ~15 months (end of S4) |
| Track capstone + portfolio package | ~18 months (end of S5) |
| Job search begins (technical/ops roles) | from end of S2 |
| Degree + SEBI registration path | ongoing in parallel |

## Next steps after the capstone

1. **Assemble the portfolio package.** One PDF combining the S2 chart
   portfolio, S3 modeled-company workbook, S4 research report, S5 capstone,
   journal samples, and certification scorecards — plus a one-page resume and a
   rehearsed 3-minute "walk me through your research" pitch. The package is your
   job interview; every deliverable in the exit list above belongs in it.
2. **Apply.** Technical/ops applications begin from end of S2 (see the timeline
   above); research/portfolio applications after the S4 report is defended and
   NISM XV is passed. Target brokerage research desks, the wealth arms of
   banks, and MF distributor firms. Mentor-network referrals and warm
   introductions consistently beat cold applications — ask for both.
3. **Intern (even briefly).** A 6–8 week internship during S5 converts capstone
   work into work experience and earns you a reference. The capstone itself is a
   work sample you can show even before the internship starts.
4. **Keep the ladder moving.** Continue or start the part-time BA/B.Com/BBA/BCA
   that keeps the SEBI RA/IA/PM registration path open, and renew NISM CPE
   credits so certifications stay valid. The credential ladder does not stop at
   the capstone — it is a career-long system.
