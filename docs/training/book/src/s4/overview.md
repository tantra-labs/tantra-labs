# Semester 4 — Quantitative Methods, Derivatives, Research & Regulation

> **Weeks 49–64 · Goal:** statistics and portfolio theory, derivatives, professional
> research output, and SEBI compliance — the final NISM XV layer.

## Modules

| Module | Title | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Statistics for Markets | Descriptive stats, distributions, skew/kurtosis |
| M2 | Correlation, Regression & ANOVA | Correlation/covariance, OLS, ANOVA, forecasting |
| M3 | Portfolio Theory: Markowitz, CAL, SML & CML | Efficient frontier, CAPM geometry |
| M4 | Performance Measurement | Sharpe, Treynor, Alpha, Beta |
| M5 | Derivatives: Forwards & Futures | Pricing, margins, basis |
| M6 | Options: Pricing & Strategies | BS intuition, greeks, put-call parity, strategies |
| M7 | Hedging & Risk Management | Portfolio hedging, position sizing, stops |
| M8 | Currency & Fixed Income Markets | FX, bonds, YTM, duration |
| M9 | Research Report Writing | Report anatomy, quality, presentations |
| M10 | Regulatory Ecosystem & Ethics | SEBI rules, codes, conflicts |
| M11 | NISM XV Mock Sprint & Final Exams | 4 mocks + final exams |

## Assessment rhythm

- Mid-term exam (Week 56) covers M1–M4.
- **Research report** (Week 62) is a programme gate.
- End-term exams + NISM XV mocks (Week 64).
