# Semester 3 — Fundamental Analysis, Valuation & Modeling

> **Weeks 33–48 · Goal:** the complete NISM XV research-analyst fundamentals
> stack, ending with a live three-statement financial model.

## Modules

| Module | Title | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Economics for Securities Markets | GDP, IIP, inflation, PMI, monetary policy; global trends |
| M2 | Industry & Company Qualitative Analysis | Top-down/bottom-up; moats; governance; MD&A; CANSLIM |
| M3 | Financial Statement Analysis | Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow deep dive |
| M4 | Ratio Analysis & DuPont | Ratio families; step-wise DuPont decomposition |
| M5 | Time Value of Money & Capital Budgeting | TVM, NPV/IRR, HPR/HPY |
| M6 | Cost of Capital, CAPM & WACC | Kd/Ke/Kp; WACC; market efficiency |
| M7 | Valuation Methods | DDM, DCF (FCFF/FCFE), EV/EBITDA, SOTP, comps |
| M8 | Financial Modeling | Revenue builders, schedules, 3-statement model |

## Assessment rhythm

- Mid-term exam (Week 40) covers M1–M4.
- **Modeled-company capstone (Week 47)** is a programme gate.
- End-term exam (Week 48): theory + 3-hour modeling practical.
