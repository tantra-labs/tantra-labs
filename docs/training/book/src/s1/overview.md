# Semester 1 — Foundations of Capital Markets

> **Weeks 1–16 · Goal:** erase the graduate-prerequisite gap and deliver complete
> market literacy. No finance or statistics background is assumed.

## Why this semester exists

Graduate curricula (like the NISM PGP source syllabus) presume you already know
basic statistics, spreadsheets, accounting, and market vocabulary. This semester
rebuilds all four **from zero**, so every later semester can assume a solid base.

## Modules

| Module | Title | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Financial Literacy & Business Math Bootcamp | 1–2 | Math for money, CAGR, percentages, demand-supply, financial calculators |
| M2 | Excel for Finance | 2–4 | Spreadsheets as a financial instrument |
| M3 | How Capital Markets Work | 4–6 | Primary/secondary markets, exchanges, IPO, world markets |
| M4 | Market Microstructure & Participants | 6–8 | Brokers, clearing, settlement, order lifecycle |
| M5 | Securities & Instruments | 8–10 | Equity, debt, hybrids, mutual funds, ETFs, NAV |
| M6 | Corporate Actions & Financial Terminology | 10–12 | Dividends, splits, buybacks; professional vocabulary |
| M7 | Introduction to Financial Statements | 12–14 | Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow; annual report |
| M8 | Introduction to Technical Analysis | 14–15 | Fundamental vs technical; first look at charts |
| M9 | Professional Skills & Markets Lab | 1–16 (continuous) | Markets news, presentations, ethics |

## Assessment rhythm

- **Mid-term exam** (Week 8) covers M1–M4.
- **End-term exam** (Week 16) covers all modules, plus a lab practical.
- Continuous assessment: quizzes, Excel worksheets, weekly markets summaries.

## Tantra tie-in

From Week 1 you use `tantra_examples.synthetic_bars` to see real (synthetic)
price series in Excel and in Python. By Week 16 you can plot a series, compute a
moving average, and explain what a stock is — you will build on this in
Semester 2.
