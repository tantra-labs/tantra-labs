# Program Learning Outcomes

On completion of this course, the learner can:

1. **PLO-1 — Market literacy.** Explain how primary/secondary markets, exchanges
   (NSE, BSE, MCX), clearing & settlement, and market participants work in India
   and globally.
2. **PLO-2 — Instruments.** Distinguish equity, debt, hybrids, mutual funds,
   ETFs, and derivatives; interpret corporate actions, NAV, and professional
   market terminology.
3. **PLO-3 — Technical analysis.** Read any chart type and candlestick, identify
   trends, support/resistance, patterns, gaps, and indicators — and **explain the
   mathematics behind each indicator** — at **CFTe I level**.
4. **PLO-4 — Trading operations.** Operate a trading terminal and a
   **Tantra `PaperSession`**, place orders, monitor positions/P&L, and keep a
   disciplined paper-trading journal.
5. **PLO-5 — Fundamental analysis.** Read income statements, balance sheets,
   cash-flow statements, annual reports, MD&A, and DRHPs; compute and interpret
   ratios; run a step-wise DuPont decomposition.
6. **PLO-6 — Economics.** Connect GDP, IIP, inflation, PMI, monetary policy,
   demand-supply, and global macro events to market behaviour.
7. **PLO-7 — Valuation.** Apply TVM, NPV/IRR, DDM, DCF (FCFF/FCFE), EV/EBITDA,
   SOTP, and relative/comps valuation; build a 3-statement financial model.
8. **PLO-8 — Quantitative finance.** Use descriptive statistics, correlation,
   covariance, regression, ANOVA, CAPM/CAL/SML/CML, and Markowitz portfolio
   theory; compute Sharpe/Treynor/Alpha/Beta.
9. **PLO-9 — Derivatives & risk.** Explain and price forwards, futures, and
   options (including put-call parity and Black-Scholes intuition); construct
   basic hedging strategies; apply position/risk management rules.
10. **PLO-10 — Professional practice.** Write a professional research report,
    deliver an analyst presentation, and operate within the SEBI legal/regulatory
    and ethics framework.

## You can prove this by

A learning outcome you cannot demonstrate is a gap, not a decoration. Each PLO
below is tied to one **deliverable** you will produce and that an assessor — or
an interviewer — can check.

| PLO | You can prove this by |
|---|---|
| PLO-1 — Market literacy | Explaining in 5 minutes, without notes, how a RELIANCE share travels from an IPO (primary market) to a secondary-market trade, through clearing and T+1 settlement. |
| PLO-2 — Instruments | Completing the S1-M5 mutual-fund worksheet: NAV arithmetic and a fund-vs-ETF comparison in correct professional terminology. |
| PLO-3 — Technical analysis | The **S2 chart-lab practical**: marking trends, support/resistance, patterns, and gaps on 10 charts — and passing **CFTe I**. |
| PLO-4 — Trading operations | A 30-day `PaperSession` fill and P&L log you can defend, plus an S2 journal entry graded "rules followed". |
| PLO-5 — Fundamental analysis | The ratio-analysis sheet and step-wise DuPont decomposition inside your S3 modeled-company workbook. |
| PLO-6 — Economics | A one-page macro brief connecting the repo rate, CPI, and IIP to a NIFTY comment, with sources cited. |
| PLO-7 — Valuation | The S3 3-statement model with a DCF sheet you can explain line by line in review (see the [S3 gate](assessment.md)). |
| PLO-8 — Quantitative finance | A portfolio lab run in `tantra`: `returns`, `mean`, `std_dev`, `covariance_matrix`, and `ols`, plus a hand-worked Sharpe/Treynor worksheet. |
| PLO-9 — Derivatives & risk | An S4 options lab that prices a call with `bs_price`, reads `bs_greeks`, recovers volatility with `implied_volatility`, and states a hedging plan. |
| PLO-10 — Professional practice | The S4 research report you submit **and defend**, plus a clean pass on the SEBI ethics/regulatory quiz. |

## Semester-by-semester checkpoint

The proof table above turns each PLO into a deliverable; this checkpoint turns
the deliverables into a calendar. At every gate, ask "where is the proof?" —
if it is not in your portfolio, that is the gap to close before advancing.

| Checkpoint | PLO proofs due | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| End of S1 | PLO-1, PLO-2 (market literacy, instruments) | S1 end-term ≥ 60% |
| End of S2 | PLO-3, PLO-4 (chart practical, `PaperSession` log + journal) | S2 end-term; CFTe I + NISM VIII window |
| End of S3 | PLO-5, PLO-6, PLO-7 (workbook, macro brief, DCF) | Modeled-company workbook rubric pass |
| End of S4 | PLO-8, PLO-9, PLO-10 (quant lab, options lab, report + defense) | S4 end-term; NISM XV window |
| End of S5 | Capstone integrating all ten PLOs | Track capstone complete |

## How to use this list

- Treat it as the **programme contract**. At the end of every semester, take the
  PLOs for that semester (mapping above) and ask "where is my deliverable?" If
  you cannot point to one, that is the gap to close before the next gate.
- Each module chapter restates the PLOs it serves, and each Tantra Lab exists to
  produce part of a proof. When a lab feels pointless, look up which PLO it feeds
  — that is its point.
- The deliverables in the table are exactly what your **portfolio package**
  (see [Career Pathways](../appendices/career-pathways.md)) is made of. Keep
  every proof; from Semester 2 on, treat each one as a job-interview artefact.
