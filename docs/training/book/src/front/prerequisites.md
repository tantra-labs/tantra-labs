# Learner Profile & Prerequisites

## Who this course is for

A student who has just finished **Class 12** (any stream — Science, Commerce, or
Arts) and wants a structured, exam-credentialed entry into Indian capital
markets without waiting for a three-year degree. No prior finance, accounting,
or statistics coursework is required.

## Prerequisite matrix (Week-0 entry gate)

| Domain | Required minimum | Gap-closing action |
|---|---|---|
| Arithmetic | Fractions, percentages, ratios, exponents, negative numbers | S1-M1 math bootcamp + nightly drills |
| Algebra | Solving a linear equation for one unknown | S1-M1 remediation labs |
| Statistics | **None required** — taught from zero | S1-M1 (descriptive stats), S4-M1 (inferential) |
| English | Basic reading/writing | Weekly communication lab (S1-M9) |
| Computing | None; mouse/keyboard comfort is enough | S1-M2 doubles as computing bootcamp |
| Finance | **None required** | Whole of Semester 1 is the bridge |
| Trading | **None required** | S2-M8 trading terminal & paper lab |

## What a high-school passout needs that a graduate has

Graduate programmes (e.g., the NISM PGP source syllabus) assume you already know:
basic statistics, spreadsheet literacy, how a company's accounts work, and the
language of markets. This course rebuilds all four in **Semester 1** before
layering the PGP-trimester content in Semesters 2–4. That "bridge + ladder"
design is what makes the course robust for a non-graduate cohort.

## Week-0 skills audit

On day one you will sit a 60-minute diagnostic covering:

1. Arithmetic & percentages (15 Q)
2. Basic algebra (10 Q)
3. Reading comprehension in English (10 Q)
4. Simple data-reading (tables and charts) (10 Q)
5. A one-page written self-introduction (essay)

The diagnostic is **not a filter** — it calibrates your remediation plan.
Scores below the line in any domain add extra lab hours in S1-M1/M2.

## The learning contract

Every learner signs a learning contract committing to:

- 30+ hrs/week of study, of which ≥ 10 are supervised labs
- Maintaining a **paper-trading journal** from Semester 2 onward
- Attempting **every certification mock** on schedule (see
  [Certification Ladder](certifications.md))
- Sitting both **mid-term and end-term examinations every semester**
  (per the IFMC syllabus discipline, which mandates mid/end-term exams)

## Realistic commitment

The programme is full-time in its default variant. For part-time, evening, or
online variants, see the adaptions table in the curriculum design document
(`docs/training/curriculum-high-school-passout.md`, §11).
