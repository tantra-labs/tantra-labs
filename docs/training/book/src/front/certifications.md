## What each exam actually tests

- **NISM Series VIII** tests order flow in equity derivatives: contracts,
  margins, open interest, and settlement — the vocabulary of the S2 labs and the
  S4-M5/M6 derivatives modules.
- **CFTe I** tests charting and analysis: 120 questions across chart
  construction, candlesticks, patterns, indicators, Dow theory, and ethics.
- **NISM Series XV** tests the research analyst's work product: statements,
  ratios, valuation, report quality, and regulation — the S3 + S4 content.
- **CFTe II** tests *constructed* analysis: producing a written chart report on
  real instruments, proctored online.
- **CMT I** tests the same canon as CFTe I with a heavier quantitative tilt —
  which is why the S4 statistics modules are part of its preparation.

**Cost planning.** CFTe/CMT fees are international and change with the issuer's
currency; NISM fees are set per exam cycle. Budget the "check current fees" line
of the comparison table into your term plan at the start of the semester before
each exam, and confirm the exact fee on the issuing body's portal at booking
time.

## Eligibility honesty (read carefully)

- **NISM examinations** are open to Class 12 passouts.
- **SEBI registration** as Research Analyst / Investment Advisor / Portfolio
  Manager requires a **graduate degree**. This course makes you exam-ready now
  and preserves the upgrade path (BA/B.Com/BBA/BCA while working, then
  register). Technical/trading/operations careers (CFTe, terminal operations,
  advisory support) are open immediately.

## Registration logistics

- NISM exams are booked via nism.ac.in / the NISM testing portal; CBT windows
  run year-round.
- CFTe I is booked via IFTA → Pearson VUE (choose date/centre at registration);
  available in English, German, Spanish, Arabic, French, Chinese.
- CFTe II runs twice a year (typically April and October), proctored online via
  Zoom.

## Frequently asked questions

1. **Can a Class 12 passout really sit these exams?** Yes — NISM examinations and
   CFTe I are open to Class 12 passouts; no degree is needed to book and pass
   them. The caveat is SEBI **registration**: RA/IA/PM registration requires a
   graduate degree, as the eligibility section above states plainly.
2. **What does that graduate-degree caveat mean for my job hunt?** You can start
   immediately in technical, trading-operations, and advisory-support roles with
   CFTe I and NISM VIII/XV passed. SEBI-registered roles (research analyst,
   investment adviser, portfolio manager) open once you add a degree — start a
   BA/B.Com/BBA/BCA part-time in parallel and the door stays open.
3. **Which exam do I book first?** NISM VIII and CFTe I at the end of S2
   (CFTe I is bookable year-round at any Pearson VUE centre); NISM XV at the end
   of S4. Do not book a certification before its sprint — the mock clinic order
   matters more than booking early.
4. **How long are NISM certifications valid?** 3 years. Renew with Continuing
   Professional Education (CPE) credits. Keep the records — renewal is a
   formality only if you keep studying.
5. **Are CFTe and CMT recognised in India?** They are international credentials
   (IFTA and CMT Association) valued by technical-analysis employers and global
   firms. They do *not* replace NISM XV for the SEBI RA route — the two ladders
   are complementary, not alternatives.
6. **What if I fail a certification exam?** Re-book. NISM CBT windows run
   year-round and CFTe I is available at Pearson VUE centres throughout the year.
   The mock clinics exist to make the re-sit unnecessary — attend them first and
   target your weakest topics.
