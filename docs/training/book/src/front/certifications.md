# Certification Ladder

The course is built **around** the exams you will actually sit. Every target exam
has a dedicated preparation sprint, and the semester content *is* the syllabus.

## Target exams

| Cert | Body | When | Pass bar | Our sprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NISM Series VIII** (Equity Derivatives) | NISM/SEBI | End S2 | ~50–60% | S2 labs + 4 mocks |
| **CFTe I** (120 MCQ, Pearson VUE) | IFTA | End S2 | 74/120 | S2 = the syllabus; 3 official-style mocks |
| **NISM Series XV** (Research Analyst) | NISM/SEBI | End S4 | ~50–60% | S3+S4 + 4 mocks |
| NISM Series IX (Merchant Banking) | NISM/SEBI | End S4 (elective) | — | S4-M10 regulatory module |
| CFTe II (applied chart report) | IFTA | Post-program (TA track) | 65/100 (per sample) | S5-TA capstone ≈ exam practice |
| CMT Level I | CMT Association | Optional, post-program | — | S5-TA elective readings |

## Eligibility honesty (read carefully)

- **NISM examinations** are open to Class 12 passouts.
- **SEBI registration** as Research Analyst / Investment Advisor / Portfolio
  Manager requires a **graduate degree**. This course makes you exam-ready now
  and preserves the upgrade path (BA/B.Com/BBA/BCA while working, then
  register). Technical/trading/operations careers (CFTe, terminal operations,
  advisory support) are open immediately.

## Registration logistics

- NISM exams are booked via nism.ac.in / the NISM testing portal; CBT windows
  run year-round.
- CFTe I is booked via IFTA → Pearson VUE (choose date/centre at registration);
  available in English, German, Spanish, Arabic, French, Chinese.
- CFTe II runs twice a year (typically April and October), proctored online via
  Zoom.

## Fees & preparation

Exam fees are the learner's responsibility. The programme provides:

- 3× timed CFTe I mock exams (based on the IFTA mock format: 120 Q, 2h30m)
- 4× NISM XV-style mocks
- weak-topic clinics after every mock
