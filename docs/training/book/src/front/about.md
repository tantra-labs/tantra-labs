# About This Course

**SEBI Capital Markets Professional (CMP)** is a robust, end-to-end course for a
**Class 12 (high school) passout** who wants a credentialed career in Indian
capital markets without first completing a three-year degree.

## What this course does

- Takes you from **zero finance/accounting/statistics** to **exam-ready and job-ready**.
- Consolidates four certification syllabi staged in `docs/training/tmp/`:
  - `NISM-PGP-PM+IA+RA.md` — trimester architecture, credits, PM/IA/RA tracks
  - `NIFM-research-analyst.md` — 39-chapter topic map
  - `IFMC-research-analyst.md` — 70-item NISM XV prep syllabus
  - `CFTe-I-II.md` — IFTA CFTe I/II and CMT program structures
- Builds every prerequisite from zero (Semester 1 is a "graduate-prerequisite bridge").
- Uses the **Tantra platform** for every hands-on lab: deterministic synthetic
  data, exact-decimal indicators, paper trading, backtesting, quant helpers, and
  options pricing — all offline and reproducible.

## Program at a glance

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Target learner | Class 12 passout, any stream; basic arithmetic assumed |
| Duration | 4 semesters × 16 weeks = 64 weeks (~14 months), plus track semester |
| Learning load | ~30 hrs/week (12 lecture + 10 lab + 8 self-study) ≈ 1,920 hrs |
| Certifications | NISM Series XV (RA), NISM Series VIII, **CFTe I**, optional CFTe II / CMT I |
| Career outcomes | Research Analyst, Technical Analyst, Investment Advisor, Portfolio support, Trade Ops |

## How the course is structured

The programme is **five blocks: four core semesters plus one track semester**.
Every semester is 16 weeks, and each one ends in an exam or a deliverable that is
also the gate to the next block.

| Semester | Weeks | Theme | Exam target at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1 — The bridge** | 1–16 | Market literacy, math bootcamp, computing, mutual funds | S1 end-term exam (gate to S2) |
| **S2 — The chartist** | 17–32 | Technical analysis and the mathematics behind it, charting, paper trading | **CFTe I + NISM Series VIII** |
| **S3 — The analyst** | 33–48 | Economics, fundamentals, valuation, financial modeling | Modeled-company workbook (gate to S4) |
| **S4 — The quant** | 49–64 | Statistics, portfolio theory, derivatives, research report | **NISM Series XV** + research report defense |
| **S5 — The track** | 65–80 | Track capstone (Technical / Advisory / Portfolio / Research tracks) | Capstone + optional CFTe II / CMT I |

Inside every core semester, **week 8 is the mid-term** and **week 16 is the
end-term**; certification mocks are scheduled in the weeks just before the target
exam. The exact weeks are in the [Certification Ladder](certifications.md) and
the marking rules in [Assessment & Grading](assessment.md).

The book is organised as **Part I (this front matter) → Parts II–V (Semesters
1–4) → Part VI (the track semester) → Part VII (appendices)**. You can read it
front-to-back, but the parts are also a reference layer you will return to —
especially the appendices.

## Book structure

The book is organized as **Parts → Chapters → Lessons**:

- **Part** = a semester (I–VII, including front matter and appendices).
- **Chapter** = a module (e.g., `S2-M4 Technical Indicators: The Math`).
- **Lesson** = the numbered `## Lesson n.n` sections inside each chapter, each
  with objectives, topics, and where relevant a **Tantra Lab**.

## The hands-on promise

Every module that can be practiced on a computer ends with a **Tantra Lab**
that uses the real `tantra` Python wheel surface and `tantra-examples` learning
scripts. See [Tantra Platform & Lab Environment](tantra-environment.md) before
starting Semester 1.

## Regulatory honesty

NISM **examinations** are open to Class 12 passouts, but SEBI **registration** as
a Research Analyst / Investment Advisor / Portfolio Manager requires a graduate
degree. This course makes you exam-ready immediately and lays out the
registration-upgrade path in [Career Pathways](../appendices/career-pathways.md).

## Who should read this book

You, if you can answer **mostly "yes"** to this 3-minute self-check. There is no
failing score — a "no" simply tells you where Semester 1 will do extra work for
you.

- [ ] You can compute a percentage without a calculator (for example, 15% of 40,000).
- [ ] You can solve `2x + 6 = 18` for `x`.
- [ ] You can say in one sentence what NIFTY or Sensex is.
- [ ] You have typed documents or used a web browser on your own for at least a month.
- [ ] You can read a one-page English newspaper article and summarise it.
- [ ] You can commit roughly 30 hours a week for 14+ months without a full-time job.
- [ ] You are willing to keep a daily journal about trades you *simulate*, never real money.

**Mostly yes (6–7):** start Semester 1 today — the quick-start list is in
[How to use this book](#how-to-use-this-book).

**Partly (3–5):** you are still exactly the right student. Do the
[Week-0 self-audit](prerequisites.md) first and expect extra lab hours in
S1-M1/M2 for the topics you rated low.

**Mostly no (0–2):** this course is still built for you — Semester 1 starts from
zero — but the Week-0 audit and the S1-M1 math bootcamp are *not optional* for
you. Follow the same path; just take the remediation seriously.

You are also the right reader if you are:

- a **career switcher** who wants the certification ladder without a degree detour;
- a **graduate student** who wants the hands-on Tantra lab layer that degree
  syllabi often lack;
- a **mentor or parent** checking what the course actually promises (see
  [Regulatory honesty](#regulatory-honesty) above).

## A note on effort and outcomes

The course works because it is honest about both sides of the bargain.

**The effort side.** ~30 hours a week for 64+ weeks is the load of a full-time
job: 12 hours of lectures, 10 hours of lab, and 8 hours of self-study every
week (see the [study-time budget](assessment.md#study-time-budget)). The single
best predictor of finishing is *consistency* — a journal that gets an entry
every day beats a brilliant burst every month.

**The outcomes side.** The course promises three concrete things, and nothing more:

1. **Exam-readiness** — you can sit and pass CFTe I, NISM Series VIII, and
   NISM Series XV on the course's own schedule.
2. **Job-readiness** — a portfolio of real deliverables (charts, a financial
   model, a research report, a track capstone) and a paper-trading journal that
   survives an interviewer's questions.
3. **A registration upgrade path** — NISM exams are open to a Class 12 passout;
   SEBI registration for RA/IA/PM roles needs a graduate degree, which you can
   earn in parallel.

It does **not** promise a trading income, a guaranteed job, or "get rich"
outcomes — anyone promising those is not selling you an education. What this
course delivers is a credential ladder and a work sample a 12th-pass student can
genuinely hold in their hand.

## What this course is not

- **Not a trading-income course.** You will never trade real money in this
  programme — every order is a `PaperSession` fill. No course can honestly sell
  "returns"; treat anyone who does as a red flag.
- **Not a degree.** What you earn is a set of professional certifications
  (CFTe I, NISM VIII, NISM XV) plus a portfolio — not a university
  qualification. If a degree is your goal, this course is the parallel track,
  not the substitute.
- **Not a shortcut.** The ~1,920 hours are real. What the course compresses is
  *when* you learn (a 12th pass, not after a B.Com) — not *how much* you learn.
- **Not a placement service.** The programme ends job-ready and
  interview-ready; it does not place you. Your portfolio, scorecards, and
  mentor network do the placing.

## How to use this book

1. Read [Prerequisites](prerequisites.md) and complete the Week-0 self-audit.
2. Set up the [Tantra environment](tantra-environment.md) and run `01_loading_data.py`.
3. Work through Parts II–VI in order; lessons assume earlier material.
4. Attempt every Tantra Lab and keep a paper-trading journal from Semester 2.
5. Track progress against [Assessment & Grading](assessment.md) and the
   [Certification Ladder](certifications.md).
