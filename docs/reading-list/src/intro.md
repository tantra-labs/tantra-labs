# Welcome & How to Use This Book

> Start here. This chapter explains what this study book is, where its 126 books
> come from, and the macro→micro method it is built on.

## What you are holding

`some-investment-books` is a curated library of **~200 files — 126 unique
titles** — spanning trading psychology, market structure, fundamental analysis,
technical analysis, trading systems, day/swing/forex styles, advanced quant and
options, market history, and biographies. In the collection the books are
arranged by *reading phase* (`00-Start-Here` → `08-Duplicates-Archive`). This
study book re-arranges the **same library by topic, from macro to micro**, so you
can study a subject end-to-end instead of following phases.

The macro→micro spine looks like this:

```text
MACRO   Part I   The whole market, money, investing philosophy
        Part II  The company (fundamentals & valuation)
        Part III The chart (price, patterns, volume)
        Part IV  The trader (psychology, systems, risk)
        Part V   Styles & instruments (day/swing, forex, options)
        Part VI  The deep end (advanced & specialized)
        Part VII The story (history & biography)
MICRO   Part VIII Beyond the market (adjacent books)
```

Every chapter names **every book on that topic**, merges their lessons into study
notes, gives a reading order and self-test questions, and — this book's signature
feature — annotates each book with a **Model refresh** note telling you how to
use a larger AI model to expand the notes from its own training data when a newer
edition exists.

## The four ways to use this book

1. **Study a topic** — pick a chapter, read the synthesis, then read the books in
   the given order. This is the "study book" mode this mdBook is built for.
2. **Follow a plan** — Appendix B assembles the books into ready-made reading
   plans (the 6-month novice plan, per-style plans, a 20-book core). Chapters
   feed the plans; plans feed the chapters.
3. **Use it as a catalog** — Appendix A is the full annotated table of all 126
   books with author, edition, rating, and a model-refresh note for every title.
4. **Study with an AI companion** — the [AI Study Companion](ai-companion.md)
   chapter explains the technique behind every "Model refresh" line: ask a
   larger model (one with a broad training corpus) to expand a chapter using its
   knowledge of the latest edition of each book. Books published before the
   model's cutoff can be refreshed chapter by chapter.

## What the ratings mean

Ratings shown as `★ 4.6` are Goodreads averages recorded in the collection's
`_info.json`. They are crowd ratings, not endorsements. Two books can share a
rating and disagree with each other — the chapter syntheses point out where.

## The collection's own warnings (kept)

The source README flags certain books as low-value or retail traps. This study
book preserves those warnings (⚠️) rather than laundering them: most **binary
options** books ([Chapter 15](part5/15-binary-options.md)), a handful of forex
"secrets" titles ([Chapter 14](part5/14-forex.md)), and
*Long-Term Secrets to Short-Term Trading* ([Chapter 11](part4/11-systems-money-management.md)).
