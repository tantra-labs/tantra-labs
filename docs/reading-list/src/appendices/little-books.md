# Appendix F — The Little Books, Big Profits Series

> The Wiley **Little Books, Big Profits** series is a curated family of
> short, entry-level investing books. This appendix catalogues the series'
> contents/topics/summaries as listed in Wiley's *Little Books, Big Profits*
> series catalogue (`little-books-asia.pdf`, wileycatalog.com), and maps each
> title onto the macro→micro chapters of this study book so you can slot the
> series into your reading path.
>
> Three series titles are **already in the main collection** (Common Sense
> Investing, That Beats the Market, Currency Trading) — they are marked below.
> The rest are companions: read the chapter first, then the Little Book as a
> quick revision pass.

## What the series is

The Little Books are designed as 150–200-page on-ramps: one idea, one author,
one weekend. Their value for this study book is not depth — the parent
chapters have that — but *clarity*: a difficult topic (valuation, hedge
funds, COT, behavioral bias) compressed to its teachable core by a
recognised practitioner. A sensible use is **chapter → Little Book**: study
the full chapter, then read the matching Little Book to fix the ideas.

## How the series maps onto the macro→micro spine

The series was not written as a curriculum, but read through this study book's
macro→micro lens it falls into the same eight parts:

- **Part I (markets & philosophy)** — *Economics*, *Market Myths*, *Sideways
  Markets*, *Commodity Investing*, *Emerging Markets*, *Main Street Money*,
  *Alternative Investments*, *Bull's Eye Investing*, *Safe Money*.
- **Part II (the company)** — *Value Investing*, *Valuation*, *That Builds
  Wealth*, *Big Dividends*, *Investing Like the Pros*, *Big Profits from Small
  Stocks*, *That Makes You Rich*.
- **Part III (the chart)** — *Stock Market Cycles* (seasonality/pattern
  timing).
- **Part IV (the trader)** — *Behavioral Investing*, *Bulletproof Investing*,
  *Stock Market Profits*, *Trading* (trend following), *Market Wizards*.
- **Part V (currencies)** — *Shrinking Dollar*, *Currency Trading*.
- **Part VI (deep end)** — *Hedge Funds*, *Venture Capital Investing*.
- **Part VIII (beyond)** — the series' cautionary/behavioral titles read as
  leisure-adjacent companions.

## Model-refresh note

The series is small, current, and republished in new editions over time (e.g.
*Common Sense Investing* was re-issued in a 10th-anniversary edition).
Because each title is short, the [AI Study Companion](../ai-companion.md)
refresh prompt works especially well here: ask your model for the latest
edition and any updated figures for the single topic of a given Little Book.

## Suggested pairing with the reading plans

Use the Little Books as the **revision layer** of
[Plan 2 · The 20-book core](reading-plans.md): after finishing a core book's
chapter, read the matching Little Book (≤1 evening each) to lock the ideas.
A complete "Little Books fast track" of the ~15 best titles takes about 3
weeks of evenings and covers the whole macro→micro spine.
