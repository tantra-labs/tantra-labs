# Appendix B — Reading Plans & Paths

> Ready-made ways to consume the 126-book library. Every plan references chapters,
> so you can dip into the study notes before committing to a book.

## Plan 1 · The 6-month novice plan (from the source README)

Follow the phases in [Appendix E](reading-list.md); this is the compressed path.

| Month | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| 1 | Phase 0 (mindset: [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md)) + **A Random Walk Down Wall Street** ([Ch. 1](../part1/01-how-markets-work.md)) |
| 2 | Phase 1 core (**Intelligent Investor**, **One Up On Wall Street** — [Ch. 2](../part1/02-investing-philosophy.md)) |
| 3 | Phase 2 basics: one TA textbook + **Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques** ([Ch. 6](../part3/06-trends-dow-theory.md), [Ch. 7](../part3/07-candlesticks.md)) |
| 4 | Phase 2 patterns (**Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns** — [Ch. 8](../part3/08-chart-patterns.md)) + open a paper-trading account |
| 5 | Phase 3 (**Come Into My Trading Room**, **Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom** — [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md)) |
| 6 | Phase 4: pick ONE style and paper-trade it with a written trading plan ([Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md) or [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)) |

After month 6: read **Evidence-Based Technical Analysis**
([Ch. 17](../part6/17-evidence-algo.md)) before risking real money, then scale from
[Ch. 16–20](../part6/16-advanced-price-action.md) as your style demands.

## Plan 3 · By style (read the general book, then the specialist books)

**Swing trader** — [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md) +
[Ch. 8](../part3/08-chart-patterns.md):
Swing Trading For Dummies → Dave Landry on Swing Trading → The Art and Science of
Technical Analysis → (aspirational) The Master Swing Trader.

**Day trader** — [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md) +
[Ch. 9](../part3/09-volume-price-action.md):
Day Trading For Dummies → How to Day Trade for a Living → Mastering the Trade →
(discipline) Trading in the Zone.

**Trend follower** — [Ch. 12](../part4/12-trend-following.md):
Way of the Turtle → Trend Following → Trend Trading for a Living → The Trend
Following Bible.

**Forex** — [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md):
Forex for Beginners → Currency Trading For Dummies → Forex Patterns and
Probabilities → Attacking Currency Trends → Inside the Currency Market.

**Value investor** — [Ch. 2](../part1/02-investing-philosophy.md) +
[Ch. 4](../part2/04-fundamentals-valuation.md):
The Intelligent Investor → The Five Rules for Successful Stock Investing →
Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits → The Essays of Warren Buffett.

**Options** — [Ch. 19](../part6/19-options.md):
Option Volatility & Pricing → Option Spread Strategies.

**Algorithmic / quant** — [Ch. 17](../part6/17-evidence-algo.md) +
[Ch. 20](../part6/20-market-philosophy.md):
Evidence-Based Technical Analysis → Algorithmic Trading → The Alchemy of Finance.

## Plan 4 · The "study with an AI companion" sprint

For each chapter you want to study:

1. Read the chapter's **study synthesis** and pick the **one** book to read fully.
2. Read the full book.
3. Run the [AI Study Companion](../ai-companion.md) **refresh prompt** on that
   book's annotation to pull in later-edition material from the model's training
   data.
4. Answer the chapter's **self-test questions**.
5. Move to the next chapter in macro→micro order (Part I → Part VIII), or switch
   to a style plan above.
