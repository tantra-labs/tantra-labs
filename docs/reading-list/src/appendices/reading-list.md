# Appendix E — The Original Curated Categorized Reading List

> The source collection's own README (`/tmp/opencode/some-investment-books/README.md`)
> is a **phase-by-phase reading path** for someone starting from zero. This appendix
> preserves that curated list in full — as a chronological curriculum that sits
> alongside this book's macro→micro topic chapters. Use the phases when you want a
> *sequence*; use the chapters when you want a *subject*.
>
> Phase folder ↔ this book: every book below links to the chapter where its study
> notes live in this book.

## Phase 0 — Fix your mind first · `00-Start-Here/`

Read in this order. This is the psychological foundation.

| # | Book | Why read it | In this book |
|---|------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Trading in the Zone** (Douglas) | The single most important trading book. Think in probabilities, not certainties. | [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md) |
| 2 | **The Disciplined Trader** (Douglas) | Discipline = following your rules even when emotions scream otherwise. | [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md) |
| 3 | **Fooled by Randomness** (Taleb) | Learn to tell luck from skill. Kills the illusion that a winning streak means you're good. | [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md) |
| 4 | **Principles — Life and Work** (Dalio) | A decision-making operating system from a legendary hedge-fund founder. | [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md) |
| 5 | **Trading for a Living** (Elder, 1993) | The classic 3-legged stool: psychology + tactics + money management. | [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md) |
| 6 | **The New Trading for a Living** (Elder, 2014) | The modern rewrite — read either 5 or 6, then use the Study Guide to test yourself. | [Ch. 10](../part4/10-psychology.md) |

> traders. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and only after Phases 2–3.

## Phase 2 — Learn to read charts · `02-Technical-Analysis/`

Start with **one** beginner textbook, then candlesticks, then patterns.

| # | Book | Why read it | In this book |
|---|------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets** (Murphy) — *or* **Technical Analysis For Dummies** | The standard TA textbook. Beginners may prefer the Dummies version first. | [Ch. 6](../part3/06-trends-dow-theory.md) |
| 2 | **Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques** (Nison, 2nd ed.) | The book that taught the West candlesticks. | [Ch. 7](../part3/07-candlesticks.md) |
| 3 | **Candlestick Charting Explained** (Morris) — *or* **Getting Started in Candlestick Charting** | Candle patterns with statistics on how often they actually work. | [Ch. 7](../part3/07-candlesticks.md) |
| 4 | **The Candlestick Course** (Bulkowski) | Workbook with quizzes — practice what you just learned. | [Ch. 7](../part3/07-candlesticks.md) |
| 5 | **Encyclopedia of Chart Patterns** (Bulkowski) | The reference desk for patterns (head & shoulders, flags, triangles…). Use it, don't read it cover-to-cover. | [Ch. 8](../part3/08-chart-patterns.md) |
| 6 | **Visual Guide to Chart Patterns** (Bulkowski) | The picture-book version of #5. Great companion. | [Ch. 8](../part3/08-chart-patterns.md) |
| 7 | **Technical Analysis Explained** (Pring, 4th ed.) | A deeper, classic TA text. | [Ch. 6](../part3/06-trends-dow-theory.md) |
| 8 | **Technical Analysis of Stock Trends** (Edwards & Magee, 9th ed.) — *or* **Technical Analysis and Stock Market Profits** | The granddaddy of TA. Dow Theory, trends, support/resistance. | [Ch. 6](../part3/06-trends-dow-theory.md) |
| 9 | **A Complete Guide To Volume Price Analysis** (Coulling) | Price + volume = the honest read of what big money is doing. | [Ch. 9](../part3/09-volume-price-action.md) |
| 10 | **The Art and Science of Technical Analysis** (Grimes) | The thinking trader's TA book — structure, context, and trade management. | [Ch. 9](../part3/09-volume-price-action.md) |
| 11 | **Trade What You See** (Pesavento) | Pattern recognition applied with Fibonacci + patterns. | [Ch. 8](../part3/08-chart-patterns.md) |
| 12 | **Naked Forex** (Rabbe) | High-probability price action without indicator clutter. | [Ch. 9](../part3/09-volume-price-action.md) |
| 13 | **Chart Your Way To Profits** (Bulkowski) | Using your charting platform properly. | [Ch. 8](../part3/08-chart-patterns.md) |

> Core lesson of this phase: indicators lag price. Learn price action and volume
> first; treat indicators as confirmation, not gospel.

| # | Book | Why read it | In this book |
|---|------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Come Into My Trading Room** (Elder) + **Study Guide** | A complete trading system: the 3 M's — Mind, Method, Money. | [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md) |

| 5 | **Mastering the Trade** (Carter, 2nd ed.) | Intraday + swing setups with rules. | [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md) |
| 6 | **Way of the Turtle** (Faith) — *or* **The Complete Turtle Trader** | The famous trend-following experiment, fully explained. A complete, public system. | [Ch. 12](../part4/12-trend-following.md) |
| 7 | **Trend Following** (Covel) — *then* **Trend Trading for a Living**, **The Trend Following Bible** | The "let winners run, cut losers short" school. | [Ch. 12](../part4/12-trend-following.md) |
| 8 | **Long-Term Secrets to Short-Term Trading** (Williams) | ⚠️ Dated & opinionated — read critically. | [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md) |
| 9 | **Building Reliable Trading Systems** (Bandy) | How to design systems that survive backtesting → live trading. | [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md) |
| 10 | **Trade Like a Pro** (Faith) | 15 concrete high-probability strategies, codified. | [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md) |
| 11 | **The Encyclopedia of Trading Strategies** | ⚠️ A smorgasbord; skip if overwhelmed. | [Ch. 11](../part4/11-systems-money-management.md) |

> Core lesson of this phase: your edge is defined by risk-reward + win rate
> (expectancy). Without position sizing and stops, no method saves you.

## Phase 4 — Pick a style · `04-Styles-Day-Swing-Forex/`

Read the general books, then specialize in **one** style.

### General / style selection
- **Swing Trading For Dummies** — gentlest intro to 2–10 day holds (best for beginners) · [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md)
- **Day Trading For Dummies** (3rd ed.) — the reality check on day trading · [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md)
- **How to Day Trade for a Living** (Aziz) — tools, tactics, money management, psychology · [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md)
- **Dave Landry on Swing Trading** — simple, teachable swing setups · [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md)
- **The Master Swing Trader** + **The Master Swing Trader Toolkit** (Farley) — ⚠️ dense; read only if swing trading clicks for you · [Ch. 13](../part5/13-day-swing.md)

### Forex (currencies)
1. **Forex for Beginners** — the on-ramp · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
2. **Currency Trading For Dummies** (2nd ed.) — solid mechanics + strategy overview · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
3. **The Sensible Guide to Forex** — the "safer, smarter" perspective · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
4. **Essentials of Foreign Exchange Trading** / **The 10 Essentials of Forex Trading** — rules · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
5. **Forex Patterns and Probabilities** — trending vs range-bound strategies · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
6. **Attacking Currency Trends** — how to trade big forex trends · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
7. **Currency Trading and Intermarket Analysis** — currencies in the cross-asset picture · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
8. **Day Trading and Swing Trading the Currency Market** / **Day Trading the Currency Market** — the older, heavier texts · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
9. **Inside the Currency Market** — valuation + mechanics for the serious forex trader · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
10. **Currency Strategy** (Lee) — practitioner-level forecasting/hedging. Advanced · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
11. **Sentiment in the Forex Market** — crowd behavior and positioning extremes · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
12. **The Little Book of Currency Trading** — a quick, honest read · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)
13. ⚠️ *Lower value:* **How to Make a Living Trading Foreign Exchange**, **Forex Trading Secrets**, **17 Proven Currency Trading Strategies**, **Forex Trading Basics & Secrets** · [Ch. 14](../part5/14-forex.md)

### ⚠️ Binary options — read with extreme caution
- **Trading Binary Options** / **Top Binary Options Trading Strategies** — these
  instruments are heavily regulated, often banned, and designed to favour the
  broker. Reading these is fine; *trading* binary options is generally not
  recommended · [Ch. 15](../part5/15-binary-options.md)

> Core lesson of this phase: pick ONE style, ONE market, ONE timeframe. Mastery
> beats variety.

## Phase 5 — Go deep · `05-Advanced/`

Read these only after Phases 0–3. They are dense, mathematical, or highly specialized.

| Area | Books | In this book |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Professional price action | **Trading Price Action Trends** (Al Brooks, bar-by-bar) → **Understanding Price Action** (5-min time frame) | [Ch. 16](../part6/16-advanced-price-action.md) |
| Validate your edge | **Evidence-Based Technical Analysis** — how to test signals with statistics before trading them | [Ch. 17](../part6/17-evidence-algo.md) |
| Institutional TA | **Technical Analysis for the Trading Professional** → **The Handbook of Technical Analysis** (reference) → **New Frontiers in Technical Analysis** | [Ch. 16](../part6/16-advanced-price-action.md) |
| Harmonic patterns | **Harmonic Trading Vol. 1** → **Vol. 2** (Fibonacci-based patterns; ⚠️ complex) | [Ch. 18](../part6/18-specialized-systems.md) |
| Ichimoku | **Trading with Ichimoku Clouds** — the all-in-one indicator system | [Ch. 18](../part6/18-specialized-systems.md) |
| Intermarket & positioning | **Intermarket Trading Strategies** → **The Commitments of Traders Bible** | [Ch. 18](../part6/18-specialized-systems.md) |
| Algorithmic trading | **Algorithmic Trading — Winning Strategies and Their Rationale** (Chan) — needs basic Python/statistics | [Ch. 17](../part6/17-evidence-algo.md) |
| Options | **Option Volatility & Pricing** (Natenberg) — the options bible → **Option Spread Strategies** | [Ch. 19](../part6/19-options.md) |
| Market philosophy | **The Alchemy of Finance** (Soros) — reflexivity theory | [Ch. 20](../part6/20-market-philosophy.md) |

> Core lesson of this phase: at this level the difference between traders is no
> longer *what setup* they use, but *how rigorously they test it* (out-of-sample,
> transaction costs, regime changes).
